About Dabeli

Dabeli is a popular street food from Gujarat, India. It's a spicy snack made with potatoes, peanuts, and spices, typically served in a pav (bread roll). The name comes from the Gujarati word 'dab,' meaning to press or stuff.

This delightful snack combines sweet, spicy, and tangy flavors that make it irresistible. It's perfect for a quick bite, lunch, or snack with friends and family.

How to Make Dabeli

Ingredients

  • 4 medium potatoes, boiled and mashed
  • ½ cup roasted peanuts, crushed
  • 2 tbsp sweet tamarind chutney
  • 1 tbsp spicy green chutney
  • 1 tsp chaat masala
  • ½ tsp red chili powder
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • Pav (bread rolls)
  • Oil for roasting

Instructions

  1. Mix mashed potatoes with peanuts and garlic
  2. Add chaat masala, red chili powder, and salt to taste
  3. Divide the mixture into portions
  4. Spread chutneys on the inner side of pav
  5. Stuff the pav with the potato mixture
  6. Roast on a griddle until golden brown on both sides
  7. Serve hot with extra chutney on the side
